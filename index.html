Our Story

A Hall Built for Grand Celebrations

Nestled in the heart of Nagpur, GK Himalaya Celebration is a premier banquet and celebration hall that transforms your most cherished moments into timeless memories. With an opulent ambiance inspired by the grandeur of the Himalayas, every event held here is nothing short of magnificent.

From intimate family gatherings to grand weddings accommodating hundreds of guests, our dedicated team ensures every detail is executed with perfection — from décor and catering to lighting and logistics.
